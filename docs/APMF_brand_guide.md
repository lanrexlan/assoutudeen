# APMF Starter Brand Guide

**Brand:** APMF — Assoutudeen Prophetic Medicine Foundation  
**Asset package:** Logo system v1.0  
**Primary use:** Healthcare, wellness, community care, education, outreach and digital communications

## Brand idea

The APMF identity combines a protective home roof with a growing medicinal plant. The roof communicates safety, shelter and care; the botanical stem communicates natural medicine, learning and renewal. The rounded outer form gives the mark a sense of protection and community.

## Typography

Use **Montserrat ExtraBold** or a close geometric sans-serif for the APMF wordmark and major headings. Use **Montserrat Medium**, **Inter**, or **Source Sans 3** for body copy, captions and digital UI. Keep the wordmark uppercase and do not stretch, condense or outline it.

## Clear space and minimum size

Use a clear-space margin around the logo equal to the height of the capital **A** in the APMF wordmark. Do not place text, faces, borders or busy visual detail inside this zone. For print, keep the full stacked lockup at least **28 mm wide**. For digital use, keep it at least **180 px wide**. Use the icon-only version below **64 px** wide.

## Approved backgrounds

The colour logo works best on warm white, white, very pale green or quiet neutral backgrounds. Use the white reversed variant on evergreen, charcoal, photography or other dark backgrounds. Never place the colour logo on a busy image without a quiet area or a solid protective panel.

## Do not

Do not rotate, skew, stretch, crop the emblem, change the roof or plant proportions, recolour the mark with unapproved colours, add drop shadows or bevels, place the logo inside an unapproved shape, or recreate the wordmark in a decorative script. Do not use the tagline when it becomes unreadable at small sizes; switch to the icon-only mark instead.

## File notes

The SVG files are self-contained and use transparent backgrounds. They use live text with a font stack for editability; for final commercial print production, convert the wordmark and tagline to outlines in the design application after the chosen brand font has been installed. The PNG master is supplied for platforms that do not accept SVG.
