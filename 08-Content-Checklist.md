# 08 — Content Collection Checklist

Print this. Work through it before a developer starts. Missing content is the single most
common reason website projects stall for months after the code is finished.

---

## Organisation facts

- [ ] Full legal name of the foundation
- [ ] CAC registration number and a scan of the certificate
- [ ] Year founded
- [ ] Registered address, and the operating office address if different
- [ ] Phone numbers — main line and WhatsApp
- [ ] Correct email address *(the current site shows `@gail.com` — verify and fix)*
- [ ] Office hours
- [ ] Bank account details for direct donations
- [ ] All social media handles: Facebook, WhatsApp, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram

## Written copy

- [ ] Mission statement — two or three sentences
- [ ] Vision statement
- [ ] Core values — four to six, each with a sentence
- [ ] The founding story — 300–500 words
- [ ] Founder biography — 300–500 words, covering Islamic and medical training, teachers,
      ijazah, years of practice, publications
- [ ] Short bios and photos for each trustee and staff member
- [ ] An explanation of how APMF, the Da'wah Institute and the Honey Enterprise relate
- [ ] "What is prophetic medicine" — 800–1,200 words, with Qur'an and hadith references
- [ ] Statement on the relationship between prophetic and conventional medicine

## Empowerment programme

- [ ] What the programme provides — be specific
- [ ] Difference between the monthly and quarterly cycles
- [ ] Eligibility criteria
- [ ] Application and selection process, step by step
- [ ] Total number of beneficiaries to date, and the start date
- [ ] Cost to sponsor one beneficiary
- [ ] For each past cycle you can document: date, location, number helped, what was given,
      total value, photos, a short report
- [ ] Three to five beneficiary stories — name, situation before, what they received, where
      they are now, photo, **signed consent**

## The book

- [ ] Title and subtitle
- [ ] Author name as it should appear
- [ ] Cover image, high resolution
- [ ] Description, 200–300 words
- [ ] Table of contents
- [ ] A sample chapter as a PDF
- [ ] Page count, language, publication year, ISBN
- [ ] Price in ₦ (and USD if selling internationally)
- [ ] Formats: paperback / PDF / EPUB
- [ ] Who fulfils orders, from where, and shipping costs by zone
- [ ] Endorsements or reviews, with permission to quote

## Da'wah Institute

- [ ] The Institute's mandate and history
- [ ] Full list of courses
- [ ] For each course: title, level, duration, format, language, module-by-module syllabus,
      learning outcomes, instructor, fee, payment plan, start dates, class times, certificate
- [ ] Admission requirements
- [ ] Academic calendar
- [ ] Scholarship policy, if any
- [ ] Instructor bios and photos
- [ ] Student testimonials
- [ ] Any accreditation or affiliation
- [ ] Existing recorded lectures — titles, descriptions, YouTube links or files

## Honey Enterprise

- [ ] Complete product list with sizes and prices
- [ ] Photographs of every product, at least four angles each
- [ ] Sourcing story — where the honey comes from, the beekeepers, the apiaries
- [ ] Purity testing and quality assurance process
- [ ] NAFDAC registration number, if obtained
- [ ] Shelf life and storage guidance
- [ ] Distributor programme: tiers, pricing, minimum orders, margins, territory rules,
      support provided
- [ ] Delivery zones and rates
- [ ] Customer reviews, with permission

## Prophetic medicine content

- [ ] List of remedies to document — aim for 10–15 at launch
- [ ] For each: Qur'an/hadith evidence with full source citation, traditional uses,
      preparation method, precautions, any research references
- [ ] Twenty frequently asked questions with answers — pull these from your Facebook
      comments and WhatsApp messages
- [ ] Consultation types, what each covers, duration, and fee

## Images

- [ ] Logo files in SVG or high-resolution PNG, all variants
- [ ] Founder portrait
- [ ] Team photographs
- [ ] Office or premises exterior and interior
- [ ] 20–40 empowerment and event photographs
- [ ] Seminar and workshop photographs
- [ ] Product photography
- [ ] Book cover
- [ ] Any certificates, awards or press coverage

## Video

- [ ] Links to existing YouTube content
- [ ] A short introduction video from the founder, if possible — this converts extremely well
      on the homepage and needs nothing more than a phone and good light

## Legal documents

- [ ] Privacy Policy *(draft from doc 06, have a lawyer review)*
- [ ] Terms of Use
- [ ] Terms of Sale
- [ ] Delivery Policy
- [ ] Returns & Refunds Policy
- [ ] Distributor Agreement
- [ ] Consultation Disclaimer
- [ ] Zakat administration policy
- [ ] Beneficiary photo and story consent form

## From Facebook — needed to finish the discovery brief

- [ ] The About section, copied in full
- [ ] Page category, tagline, follower count, creation date
- [ ] Your five to ten best-performing posts
- [ ] All empowerment event photo albums
- [ ] Recurring questions from comments and messages
- [ ] Any reviews or recommendations left on the page

---

## Suggested order of work

1. Fill in the organisation facts — one afternoon
2. Commission the photography — it takes the longest to arrange
3. Write the empowerment content — it's your strongest asset and the hardest to fake
4. Assemble the book materials
5. Write the remedies library — this is the largest writing job; do a few each week
6. Everything else

Assign a single named person to own this checklist with a weekly deadline. Content collected
by committee is content never collected.
