# 03 — Page-by-Page Content Plan

This is the blueprint a designer and developer will build from. Each page lists its purpose,
its sections in order, and what content you must supply.

---

## Homepage (`assoutudeen.com`)

**Purpose:** in about eight seconds, tell a visitor who you are, prove you're real, and give
them one obvious next step.

**Sections in order:**

1. **Hero.** A single strong photograph — the founder teaching, or an empowerment
   distribution in progress. Not a stock image of honey.
   - Headline: something like *"Healing by the Sunnah. Empowering the Ummah."*
   - Subline: one sentence naming what you do and where
   - Two buttons: `Donate` (primary) and `Explore Prophetic Medicine` (secondary)
2. **Trust bar.** Four numbers: years active · people empowered · seminars held ·
   consultations given. Numbers must be real; if you don't have them, count them.
3. **What We Do.** Four cards — Prophetic Medicine, Empowerment, Da'wah Education,
   Pure Honey. Each links to its section.
4. **The Empowerment Programme.** A wide feature block. Photos from the most recent cycle,
   a short story about one beneficiary, the cycle date, and two buttons:
   `Apply for Support` / `Fund the Next Cycle`.
5. **The Book.** Cover image, title, one-line pitch, price, `Read a Sample` and `Buy Now`.
6. **From the Foundation.** Three most recent articles.
7. **Our Family.** Three panels linking to APMF, the Da'wah Institute, and the Honey
   Enterprise — this is where visitors learn the group structure.
8. **Testimonials.** Three real ones, with names and photos where consent allows.
9. **Newsletter + WhatsApp.** Email capture and a WhatsApp community link.
10. **Footer.**

**You supply:** hero photo, real statistics, one beneficiary story, book cover and blurb,
three testimonials with consent.

## About / Who We Are

Mission and vision in plain language · the founding story and the year · the problem you
exist to solve · your values grounded in Qur'an and Sunnah with citations · geographic reach
· a photo of the team or office · CAC registration number displayed openly.

## The Founder

Full biography, Islamic and medical education, teachers and ijazah if applicable, years of
practice, publications, speaking engagements, a good portrait photograph. Donors and
patients are trusting a person before they trust an institution — this page carries more
weight than the About page.

## Our Structure

A simple diagram showing APMF at the top with ADI and AHE beneath, and one paragraph on
each. State plainly whether AHE profits support the foundation's charitable work.

## Accountability

CAC certificate (image or PDF) · board of trustees with names and roles · annual report PDF
for each year you have one · a breakdown of how donations are spent · bank account details
for direct transfer · a named contact for financial questions. This page is the difference
between a diaspora donor giving ₦5,000 and giving ₦500,000.

## Appeals (the flagship section)

APMF's model is **public fundraising appeals for sick and vulnerable Muslims, followed by a
full public accounting.** Build for that, not for a grants programme.

### Appeals index
Two tabs: **Active** and **Closed**. Each card shows the beneficiary (or "a sister who wishes
to remain anonymous"), the need in one line, a progress bar, ₦ raised against ₦ target, and
days open.

### Individual appeal page
The most important template on the site.

- Beneficiary name or anonymised label · photo if consented
- The need, told properly — the 2023 entries show the format: nature of illness, hospital,
  circumstances
- **Target amount · amount raised · progress bar · number of donors**
- Status: Active / Closed / Partially Met
- Donate widget inline, with suggested amounts
- **"Share on WhatsApp" button that pre-fills a formatted broadcast message** — your
  distribution already runs on WhatsApp rebroadcast, so make the site feed it. This will
  likely drive more donations than every other feature combined.
- Updates timeline — disbursement, surgery date, outcome
- Closing report when the appeal ends

**Publish shortfalls.** ₦3,035,000 raised against a ₦22,000,000 kidney-transplant target is
not something to hide; it is proof you report reality. Organisations that only show
successes get trusted less, not more.

### Accountability Reports (one page per fiscal year)
Reproduce your existing broadcast format as a proper web page: the At-Tawbah 9:105 verse, the
du'a for donors, the full beneficiary table with amounts, the surplus-fund assistances, and
the standing offer of bank statements on request. 2023 is written already — publish it as
the template and add 2024, 2025, 2026.

Show a headline total: **₦5,323,500 raised across 11 beneficiaries in 2023.**

### Request Assistance (form)
Name · phone · WhatsApp · state and LGA · nature of need (medical / financial / shelter /
other) · hospital and diagnosis if medical · amount required · supporting documents upload
(hospital bill, diagnosis) · referee name and phone · whether they consent to being named
publicly. Submissions land in an admin dashboard for verification, not an inbox.

### How Appeals Work
Explain your process: how a case reaches you, how you verify it, how funds are held, how
they are disbursed (direct to hospital where possible), what happens to surplus, and how you
report. This page answers the unspoken question every donor has.

## Prophetic Medicine section

**What Is Prophetic Medicine** — an authoritative explainer with Qur'an and hadith
references, the distinction between prophetic medicine, ruqyah, and general herbal
medicine, and a clear statement on where conventional medicine fits. Say explicitly that
you do not tell people to abandon prescribed treatment. This protects you legally and
raises your standing with educated readers.

**Remedies Library** — a filterable index, built directly from the book's chapters. Each
remedy page follows the book's own structure, which is already excellent:

1. Arabic name and transliteration (e.g. *Habbatus Saudā* / الحبة السوداء)
2. The Qur'anic verse, if one applies, in Arabic with translation and reference
3. The hadith evidence, in Arabic with translation and full source citation
4. Ibn al-Qayyim's commentary from *At-Tibb an-Nabawiyy*
5. Nutritional composition
6. Traditional uses
7. Precautions
8. The standard disclaimer
9. A link to the book, and to the honey shop where relevant

Publish 10–15 chapters free; the rest stay in the book. **These pages will be the bulk of
your organic search traffic** — "black seed benefits in Islam", "zamzam water benefits",
"benefits of miswāk", "talbina for grief" are all searched heavily and poorly served.

Render the Arabic properly: `dir="rtl"`, `lang="ar"`, Noto Naskh Arabic, diacritics intact.
Getting this wrong in front of this audience is worse than omitting the Arabic entirely.

**Articles & Research** — a blog, categorised and tagged, with author bylines and dates.
Aim for two posts a month, minimum.

**FAQ** — pull the real questions from your Facebook comments and WhatsApp.

**Book a Consultation** — consultation types and fees, how it works (in person, phone,
video), a booking form with date and time preference, payment if you charge, and a clear
note about what you can and cannot help with.

## The Book — *Endless Blessings From The Creator*

**Author:** Abd'Waasi Tirmidhi A. (Abu Mubaashir) · **175 pages** · **~45 remedies**

Cover image large · title and subtitle · the pitch: *"Forty-five natural remedies from the
Qur'an and the Sunnah, each traced to its evidence and explained"* · what the reader will
learn in five to seven bullets · **the full table of contents** (it is genuinely impressive —
olive oil, black seed, honey, zamzam, talbīna, miswāk, sidr, 'oud and forty more) · a free
sample chapter as a PDF in exchange for an email address · about the author · scholarly
endorsements if obtainable · price in ₦ and USD · formats (paperback / PDF / EPUB) ·
delivery time and cost by zone · reviews · FAQ · a sticky `Buy Now` bar on mobile.

**The strategy that matters:** publish 10–15 chapters free as the Remedies Library (see
below) and keep the rest for buyers. Those free chapters are simultaneously your SEO engine
and your best sales pitch — a reader who finds your black-seed page through Google, sees the
footnoted hadith citations and the Ibn al-Qayyim commentary, needs no further convincing that
the book is worth buying.

Add a `Bulk Order Enquiry` link for masjids and Islamic schools.

## Donation flow

Give donors preset amounts (₦1,000 / ₦5,000 / ₦25,000 / ₦100,000 / custom), a one-time vs
monthly toggle, and a purpose selector: Empowerment Fund, Sadaqah Jariyah, Zakat, Ramadan
Food Drive, General, Da'wah Institute Scholarships.

**Handle Zakat separately and carefully.** Zakat has strict eligibility rules for
recipients. State plainly how you administer zakat funds, who is eligible under the eight
categories, and confirm that 100% of zakat goes to eligible recipients with administration
covered from other funds. Nigerian and diaspora Muslim donors will look for exactly this.

Post-donation: an immediate receipt by email, a thank-you page, and an invitation to join
the WhatsApp community. Follow up later with a report showing what their gift did.

## Da'wah Institute pages (`dawah.assoutudeen.com`)

**Home** — the hero is a live "Next class" widget computed from the recurrence rules, then a
this-month calendar, then the seven programme cards.

**Schedule** — the centrepiece. A generated calendar for all seven programmes, with
"This week" / "This month" views and a downloadable `.ics` feed so attendees subscribe once
and never miss a session. Because every programme is rule-based, this page maintains itself.

**One page per programme** (seven): what it covers, teacher, recurrence in plain words
("every second Saturday"), time, venue or platform, language, cost, past recordings, and a
"Join the WhatsApp group" / "Add to calendar" button.

**Teachers** — full bios and photographs for Imam Engr. Abd'Waasi Tirmidhi (Abu Mubaashir)
and Shaykh Yaaqub Muhibullah Abd'hammed Olore.

**Library** — recorded lectures grouped by programme and date, with notes and reading lists.

**About · Contact.**

## Honey Enterprise pages (`honey.assoutudeen.com`)

**Home · Shop grid · one page per product · Cart & Checkout** — pricing by litre and pack
size; the existing wholesale model has a 5 L minimum qualifying order, so show retail and
wholesale tiers clearly.

**Our Honey** — sourcing and apiaries · purity and testing · **How to Identify Fake Honey**
(high-traffic Nigerian search, and it substantiates your "100% original" claim) · storage
and usage · the An-Nahl 16:68–69 framing from the book, presented as education.

**Ambassador Programme** — formalise the July 2026 contest:
- How It Works — register, receive a code like `HONEY-KEMI`, share it, earn
- **Live Leaderboard** — automated from confirmed orders, replacing the manual Facebook
  updates. Show rank, ambassador name, volume in litres, and distance to the next tier.
- Register as Ambassador (form)
- Ambassador dashboard (login) — my code, my referred orders, my volume, my rank
- Rules & Terms — carry over the existing rules: new customers only, one code per customer,
  minimum order, prizes only awarded if the volume threshold is met
